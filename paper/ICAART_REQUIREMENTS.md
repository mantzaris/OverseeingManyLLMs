# ICAART 2027 submission preparation

Verified 2026-09-10 from the official [guidelines](https://icaart.scitevents.org/Guidelines.aspx?y=2027), [templates](https://icaart.scitevents.org/Templates.aspx?y=2027), [FAQ](https://icaart.scitevents.org/FAQ.aspx?y=2027), [AI policy](https://icaart.scitevents.org/AiTools.aspx?y=2027), and [call](https://icaart.scitevents.org/CallforPapers.aspx?y=2027).

Regular review submissions use PDF, **10,000--50,000 non-whitespace characters**, including references, tables, figures and appendices. Double-blind review requires removing author identities and identifying acknowledgements. Accepted full papers receive **12 pages**, short papers eight; references and appendices count. Extra pages can incur fees. We target a main manuscript within the ordinary full-paper allowance. The currently stated regular deadline is 15 September 2026, with a later position/regular round on 22 October. Nothing is submitted or published by this session.

The official ZIP is `https://www.scitepress.org/documents/SCITEPRESS_Conference_Latex.zip`, linked directly from the 2027 template page. Its unchanged style, bibliography style and class files are under `template/`; archive and file hashes are in `artifacts/stage6_robustness/sources/template_provenance.json`. The example requests A4, a single-column title/abstract, two-column body, Times text, 10-point body, 9-point captions/references, title-cased title/keywords and uppercase section headings. Abstract length is 70--200 words. No headers, footers or page numbers are added. Main figures are vector PDFs. Template files are not edited.

No explicit regular-paper supplementary-upload policy was found in the official guidelines, FAQ or template. Their optional complementary-material language concerns abstracts, not regular submissions. `supplement.pdf` is therefore a reviewable local companion, with no assertion that it may be uploaded or will be reviewed. The main manuscript contains its essential methods, assumptions, results and limitations.

The AI policy requires naming the system, identifying affected content and disclosing how it was used. The general guidelines also request a citation in each section containing AI-generated text. Brief section-level footnote credits satisfy this request without presenting the AI tool as scientific evidence. It also assigns responsibility to human authors and excludes AI from authorship. The guideline asks for acknowledgement-section disclosure, while the double-blind rule removes acknowledgements; the AI page permits an appropriate section. The anonymous draft therefore uses a nonidentifying **AI Assistance and Reproducibility** section, cites the system and describes assistance throughout the text and code. It does not falsely say that AI was used only for proofreading. Confirm disclosure placement in the submission workflow before upload; do not omit it.

The conference also restricts public posting during review. No repository push, paper upload, preprint posting, contact, registration, payment or submission is performed. Human authors must approve authorship, responsibility, conflicts/funding declarations, originality and venue submission choices. These cannot be inferred or supplied by the coding agent.
