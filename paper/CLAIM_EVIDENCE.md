## Scope of the contribution

The framework contribution is reproducible integration of actual LLM proposals and feedback histories with a public/private information boundary, finite simulated review time, deadline-dependent intervention value and explicit cost/count scoring. The empirical contribution is the demonstrated dependence on workload and capacity, the separation of prediction quality from allocation benefit, and the objective tradeoff with a structural null control. [RESEARCH_DRAFT.md](RESEARCH_DRAFT.md) develops those arguments; [RELATED_WORK.md](RELATED_WORK.md) verifies primary-source positioning against KnowNo, constrained deferral and established scheduling research.

Claims deliberately excluded: novelty of exhaustive scheduling, cost-sensitive deferral or scalarization; measured human attention or diagnostic performance; general LLM alignment; real maintenance effectiveness; full-horizon optimality; equal outcomes across agent IDs; an exact Pareto frontier; independent-scenario treatment of repeated policy/job/prompt rows; counterfactual gains inferred from Brier scores alone; and bitwise regeneration from equal seeds.
