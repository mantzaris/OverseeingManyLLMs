# Adaptive acquisition and transfer of inspected corrections

The question is whether purchased feedback improves distinct sibling outputs enough to justify selective transfer and adaptive inspection. Acquisition and transfer are separate choices. Annotation feedback is idealized supervision; generated sibling repairs can fail or damage correct answers. No all-output private score can update the online policy.

Development uses the first twelve eligible released training contexts. Early versions, poor answers and interrupted requests are retained. v1 exposed scale-default errors. v2 puts reasoning before the answer and shows the inspected answer's original and corrected form. Its first eight groups include both natural helpful and harmful transfer. v3 tests ordinary Markdown tables and an explicit calculator request (answer beginning with '='); intermediate calculations must not replace a requested year or descriptive answer. This is interface development, shared by every eventual method, not a targeted advantage.

The provisional design compares individual correction, same-context correction memory, fixed evidence compatibility, adaptive acquisition/transfer, and a fixed audit order with the same learned transfer mechanism. A no-correction reattempt controls for extra generations. Each episode uses the original distinct human-written questions in one source context. Budgets 0/1/2 are recorded as prefixes of the same budget-independent rule; a budget-3 diagnostic may be included if the forecast fits. Source context, not an individual question or agent, is the statistical unit. Public test-gold labels are available; the final source-order subset and replicas will be committed after the pilot, before evaluation generation.

The learner is initialized only on development. It estimates wrong-answer risk and conditional beneficial/harmful repair rates from observable question and evidence relations. During an episode it can update a repaired sibling's transition only if that sibling is subsequently inspected. The official reference metric is then applied to that question's saved before/after states. Merely sharing a context is not a gold applicability label. True applicability and repair competence are not separately identifiable from outcome transitions; the report must say so.

Success is improved held-out correctness at the same inspection budget against competent fixed methods, with harm and computation reported separately. A gain solely from direct annotation replacement, parsing changes, additional inference or a larger recipient budget is not a transfer advance. If simple sharing or individual correction suffices, recommend that finding rather than a new algorithmic name.
