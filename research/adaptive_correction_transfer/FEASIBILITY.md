# Development feasibility and decision

Twelve fixed source-order training contexts contain 72 original questions. All pilot requests, failures, earlier interfaces and outcomes are retained. No held-out generation was used in development.

The final interface (v4) achieved 20/72 initial official exact matches and 48/72 exact scale matches. This is limited individual competence. Of 60 sibling transfers, nine repaired an incorrect answer and six damaged a correct answer under the final format-only acceptance guard. Three proposed repairs were malformed. Matched fresh reattempts without a correction repaired seven and damaged three. Thus natural correction transfer exists, but these development results do not show a net advantage over another generation.

Observable relation bins are sparse. Evidence-linked transfer had 3 repairs and 1 harm in 7 trials; lexical transfer 4 and 3 in 21; context-only transfer 2 and 2 in 32. These data justify an uncertainty-sensitive empirical test, not a claim that citations certify applicability. We use pooled shrinkage, a maximum of three selective recipients, and an explicit harm term. No calibrated applicability or safety claim is made.

Earlier interface outcomes remain in `development_summary.json`. v1 used an overly implicit calculator interface, v2 changed reasoning order and made scale instructions explicit, v3 changed table formatting and made calculator invocation explicit. v4 removes the recipient's prior answer from the fresh re-solution prompt to reduce anchoring. All final methods receive this same interface. v2 raw proposals included three harmful transitions, while the final format guard would retain the previous answer for one, yielding two accepted harmful transitions. This explains the difference from the contemporaneous development log.

Decision: proceed with the declared adaptive-acquisition comparison and strong fixed/no-correction controls. There is no justification for adding more tasks chosen for favorable transfer, supplying hidden scope labels, or continuing interface tuning. If adaptive inspection does not beat fixed inspection with the same transfer machinery, its additional complexity will not be presented as an advance. No alternate dataset or intervention portfolio is launched.

The sequential-controller smoke pilot reused the first four development contexts with new common initial generations and all seven policies. It established nonidentical audit decisions and purchased-label updates after a later inspection of a repaired answer. Its complete per-method outcomes are in `development_protocol_summary.json`. This is reused development material, not independent validation. No algorithm or hyperparameter change followed this pilot.

The freeze selects 24 untouched labeled test contexts, 144 questions, two generation replicas, seven core policies and two inspections. Twelve replica-zero contexts additionally compare a frozen parameter model; eight compare a third inspection. The worst-case evaluation is 1,992 scheduled generations. Measured development mean latency was 1.84 seconds; a conservative 1.5x margin forecasts 91.5 minutes. The hard inference cutoff remains authoritative. No replacements or outcome-dependent sample expansion are permitted.
