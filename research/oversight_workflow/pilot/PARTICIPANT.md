# Task instructions

You will inspect questions about financial tables and their original source text.
Some blocks include model answers. Another block asks you to answer directly from
the source. Model answers and explanations can be wrong, incomplete or absent.
Use the original source to check them. You do not need to finish every question.

- **Approve** records that you accept this answer version. It is not yet released.
- **Correct** records and approves your own answer version. Enter one value or
  text span per line, and choose its scale.
- **Release this version** completes the release of the approved answer.
- **Reject** blocks the answer. It does not count as a correct completed answer.
- **Defer** leaves the question visible and saves your note and uncommitted draft.
  You may select it again; the source and your draft return.

Check the question's period and units. Years and counts do not acquire a monetary
scale because they appear in a financial table. A percentage answer should be
entered as the number of percent and the `percent` scale. For example, twelve
percent is `12` with `percent`, not `0.12` with `percent`. This is an entry convention,
not an answer to any scored question. Choose `million`, `thousand` or another
listed scale only when supported. Preserve meaningful signs. If wording or source
information is ambiguous, you may leave a note, defer or reject rather than guess.

The calculator accepts numbers, parentheses and `+`, `-`, `*`, `/`, and rounds the
result to two decimals. It cannot decide what the numbers mean. Percentage change
and difference are different operations; use the question and evidence to choose.
Do not use external search or another assistant.

Your active source and answer remain stable when new work arrives. The pending
list shows what remains. The source-session interface additionally lets you keep
up to three questions with one source together, and pause/resume new task starts.
In-flight tasks can still arrive. Paused, rejected and unfinished work stays counted.
One question's approval never approves another answer.

For the source-only block, write your answer from the source, record it and then
release it. There is no model proposal to approve. You can also leave it unanswered.

The timer ends the block. You cannot approve or release after that point, but
unfinished work and recorded actions are preserved. Tell the investigator about
technical problems. A lost browser connection does not pause the timer. You may
end a block or withdraw; use the investigator's instructions for the applicable
withdrawal and data-handling process.

After a block, report your own experience. Questionnaire entries have no default
answer. You may leave an item unanswered. The investigators are testing task
feasibility and interface usability, not assuming one interface will be better.

This sheet is task instruction. It is **not** a consent form or a statement of
institutional approval.
