# Matched comparison preparation report

**Result:** `matched-qgm-v1` is a runnable, frozen prospective comparison. It can
now test the proposed grouping effect without the prior admission-control confound,
and draft assistance against manual work under matched allocations. It has **no
participant result**. The current position-paper manuscript remains unchanged.

## What changed

The new adapter uses the existing protocol, original source views, uniform answer
display, calculator, deferred drafts, versioned decisions, separate release and
logging. Q and G have identical saved drafts, interfaces and actions apart from
G's optional source cards and source-aware Next. Admission controls are hidden and
refused in every condition. M uses Q's queue and answer-entry/release workflow,
with no model proposals or generated explanations loaded.

All three conditions now have twelve offered questions and nine minutes with the
same two-wave start schedule. Three disjoint packets rotate over the six possible
condition orders and three packet rotations. Training remains a separate source.
The old B/C/S formative-v1 version, outputs, declarations and paper are preserved.

The primary frozen endpoint is the mean participant difference **G minus Q** in
joint-correct current-version releases divided by all 12 offered questions. It
keeps incorrect releases/approvals, blocked and unfinished work, missed blocks and
scale errors visible. Q-M and G-M are secondary assistance comparisons. No
noninferiority or powered superiority claim is declared.

## Material and task suitability

The new version reuses 36 original TAT-QA questions from six financial-report source
contexts, assigned as three twelve-question packets, with replica-0 genuine model
drafts in assisted conditions. Source tables, paragraphs and wording are unchanged.
The pilot's seven restored answer displays are retained uniformly. Historical exact
match stays **61/290**; historical empty outputs stay **38**, while the pilot display
projection separately has **31 empty or unsupported fields**.

The selected drafts are only 7/36 joint-correct and five are empty/unsupported.
Participants may need substantial reconstruction. This is why matched manual work
is necessary. Packets were retained using their previous public-characteristic
selection, not chosen for a grouping advantage. New independent model/task data
were not collected, and these previously inspected sources are not six identified
independent financial reports.

## Behavioral explanation and its limits

The logs distinguish source-focus events from returning after selection of another
source. They retain group membership/use, deferred review intervals, release events
and unrelated-request waits during source-group review. A neutral post-task
interview asks how people used sources, drafts and controls. It does not teach the
hypothesized grouping benefit before tasks or require think-aloud during the clock.
An empty event-linked coding form supports inspection of negative cases and
alternative explanations. There are no quotes or identified themes yet.

Source openings and elapsed intervals are proxies. Even fewer source returns plus
higher correct releases would require careful interpretation of task, order, draft
quality and self-selected group use. Comparing distinct packets within participant
prevents answer memory but leaves packet effects that counterbalancing and coverage
reports address only within this limited source sample.

## Actual collection boundary

Metadata inspection found six old software exports, one old investigator-practice
export and this stage's one software export. No genuine matched participant records
or documented collection authorization were found in the inspected repository and
known pilot locations. That does not assert anything about private off-system
investigator records. Old practice is excluded from new comparative analysis.

Participant mode requires the matched version/hash, an investigator authorization
record and an external assignment-schedule reference. The record is an assertion
of actual authorization, not approval created by the software. A participant-run
analysis cannot silently mix fixtures, practice, old versions or mismatched inputs.
The human-results file remains unpopulated.

## Claim and next investigator action

The stronger claim now being tested is that optional same-source review increases
correct releases within the same review period compared with a competent queue.
Matched source-only work tests whether these drafts provide useful assistance.
Neither claim is established yet. The existing systems position remains the
supported paper contribution. No manuscript wording was strengthened and no new
algorithmic advantage is asserted.

**Next action:** the responsible investigator should register the first authorized
matched tranche and code-to-assignment schedule, then run that comparison. This
includes ensuring that the existing institutional/consent arrangements actually
cover this changed protocol. It is one collection handoff, not another automated
study. Actual observations should determine whether to revise the interface and
whether the paper can claim grouping or assistance benefits.
