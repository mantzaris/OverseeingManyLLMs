# Neutral retrospective interview and event-linked interpretation

Conduct after all timed blocks and questionnaires. Do not describe the expected
source-reconstruction benefit before tasks. No concurrent think-aloud is part of
this version. Record notes or audio only as actually authorized and consented.
Identify the person by study code and retain the consent/data boundary.

Ask in this order, allowing concrete examples:

1. How did you decide what to work on next in each block?
2. How did you use the table and paragraphs? Was anything difficult to locate?
3. When a draft was present, what did you do with it? Describe a case where it
   helped, did not help, or misled you, if any.
4. Did you use the optional source sessions? What led you to use or avoid them?
5. What happened when you left a question and later returned? Was anything lost or
   difficult to recover?
6. Did working on one question change how you approached another? Give an example,
   including any case in which an assumption did not carry over.
7. Was any pending question easy to overlook or delayed while you worked elsewhere?
8. Was it clear which answer version would be released? Which controls or states
   were unclear, and what concrete defect prevented completion?

Do not require a favorable and unfavorable example when the person has none. Do
not manufacture quotes, fill in answers from logs, or interpret silence as a theme.
A facilitator may show the person's own recorded request after the timed blocks to
clarify an account, recording that prompt and its event ID. This is retrospective
support, not additional timed task performance.

## Qualitative procedure declared before collection

Two roles are useful when available: an analyst creating an initial event-linked
account and another checking interpretations/negative cases. If only one analyst
is available, say so. Do not claim inter-rater reliability without an actual method
and observations.

Read every available account, then link concrete strategies/failures to the
`qualitative_links_blank.csv` template and original event sequence. Starting
sensitizing categories are source return, within-source continuation, deferral and
resumption, draft acceptance/reconstruction, carryover assumption, unrelated-task
wait, and version/release confusion. They are questions to investigate, not observed
themes. Add emergent categories transparently and preserve an analysis log.

For each pattern retain person/condition/packet/request identifiers, the supporting
utterance or facilitator note, event times, analyst, alternative explanation and
negative case. Distinguish an actual quote, a note paraphrase, a logged act and an
analyst inference. Report participant counts and representative contrary cases,
not numbers of clicks as people. Pair claims about benefit with final answer/error
outcomes. Self-selected grouping use and elapsed times do not establish mediation,
reading time or cognitive effort. Any interface change motivated by this work
creates a new development version before a later effectiveness study.
