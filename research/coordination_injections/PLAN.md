The application is a four-role analytics team. A stock-code aggregate feeds a chart,
whose displayed values feed numerical report claims. A separately scoped appendix
must retain its original definition. UCI Online Retail provides real recorded
transactions. Briefs, scopes, changes, roles and disturbances are constructed.

First establish SQL, chart and claim competence on December 2010 and January 2011
development blocks with complete current instructions. Permit two development
revisions, preserving failures. A simple exact analysis contract, public execution
probes and independently evaluated real-data outputs prevent agreement from being
used as correctness. If the role interfaces remain unreliable, narrow their output
format once and report the resulting limits.

Investigate global shared-state repair, direct broadcast, targeted correction and
limited propagation followed by reconciliation. All see the current authorized
instructions and have the same checks and repair caps. Broadcast and targeted use
the same correction packet to isolate routing. The global baseline checks every
artifact and repairs only invalid work. No baseline is deliberately denied updates.
An exact parameterized pipeline will expose how much of this application needs an
LLM at all. Planning and packet routing are deterministic and are not novel merely
because they resemble flocking.

Provisional evaluation: 16 projects over eight disjoint monthly data blocks,
February through September 2011, two replicas, four methods. Six change templates
cover country, metric, period, customer inclusion, signed line value and chart
display size. These are dependent projects from one retailer, not 16 independent
organizations. The exact matrix and statistical analysis will be frozen and
committed after competence and throughput checks and before held-out generation.
No source or model outcome will select favorable cases. Synthetic edge cases and
matched controlled disturbances will remain secondary.

The hypothesized benefit is correct adaptation at lower communication cost while
preserving exceptions. The strongest competing explanation is ordinary dependency
invalidation plus a deterministic execution guard. A global check or parameterized
pipeline matching the method is a legitimate result. We will report artifact
correctness, accepted outputs, unfinished work, scope preservation, calls and
messages separately, rather than treating a weighted coordination score as success.
