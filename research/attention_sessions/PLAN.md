## Question and mechanism

Can a small, editable context session reduce modeled repeated setup and session
offers while preserving opportunities that a simple EDF continuation could
still complete? The intervention is a public certificate attached to a bounded
session, plus an unresolved queue. It is not automatic answer propagation,
novel family batching or a claim about mental demand. Literature comparison
precedes selection; the method and all comparisons are frozen before the main
CPU replay. Historical evaluation labels have already been inspected, so all
new results are exploratory even when configuration is locked.
