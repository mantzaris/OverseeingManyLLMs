# Figure guide

All scientific figures are generated by `plot.py` from the saved main tables and
traces. Each basename has PDF, SVG and 300-dpi PNG exports under
`artifacts/attention_sessions/figures/`. Font sizes and color palette are shared.
Images were inspected, including the unfavorable timeline and actual interface.
These figures belong to the exploratory research branch, not the submitted paper.

| Figure | Caption and interpretation |
|---|---|
| `quality_effort` | Quality versus modeled active review time, six constructed bundles of measured-source windows and saved model outputs. Left: assumed ideal review. Right: saved fallible review with misspecified risk-only admission. Circles are nondominated mean loss/time pairs; crosses indicate mean domination, not an inferential test. No human effort is measured. |
| `quality_and_loss` | Mean correct diagnoses and weighted unresolved error under the primary constructed ideal-review condition. Whiskers are conditional 95% whole-bundle bootstrap intervals, 2,000 resamples. Six bundles on one apparatus do not identify population uncertainty. Labels are externally annotated; weighted consequences are assumed. |
| `paired_differences` | Every source-bundle difference for guarded minus sticky EDF (primary) and ordinary benefit greedy (secondary). Negative favors guarded. Correctness uses source labels and fixed proposals; review timing and ideal correction are simulated. The unfavorable primary comparison is retained. |
| `demand_capacity` | Loss and session offers across declared active budgets and low/high arrivals. The demand index is nine times (setup 1 + decision 1), divided by budget, excluding switching and overlap savings. It is a transparent standalone-demand index, not observed human demand. Curves join the three declared conditions, not optimized operating points. |
| `ablations` | Guarded-minus-sticky differences after one-factor changes in constructed context, duration and grouping semantics. Source proposals and labels stay fixed. Zero-context-cost and unrelated-context results limit the claim. Values are descriptive means; absence of a bar is a tie, not missing data. |
| `timeline_unfavorable` | First numerical primary bundle where guarded loses to sticky EDF (bundle 0). Gray setup/switch/coordination precedes each colored decision. Triangles are arrivals, crosses cutoffs, circles completions, S labels session offers. All dynamics are simulated over historical diagnoses. Coordination consumes an opportunity available to sticky EDF. |
| `timeline_tied` | First numerical primary tie (bundle 1), same legend and fixed-input comparison. No favorable primary bundle exists; the absent category is recorded explicitly. |
| `timeline_secondary_benefit` | First numerical benefit against original expected-benefit greedy, explicitly a secondary comparator. It is not substituted for the absent favorable primary case. Same simulated-dynamics and saved-output interpretation as other timelines. |
| `interface_initial`, `interface_active`, `interface_deferred`, `interface_grouped` | Actual screenshots of the local prototype, captured by a scripted browser. They show public evidence, individual decisions, visible deferred requests and a bounded session. These are development demonstrations, not user-study evidence. |

Recommended reading order is quality/effort, paired differences, unfavorable
timeline, then sensitivities. Tables contain all 75 conditions. Showing a
compact subset here does not omit any raw outcome from the package.
