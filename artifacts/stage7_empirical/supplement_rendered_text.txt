Keywords:        Reproducibility, Review Semantics, Paired Evaluation.

Abstract:        This companion records experimental matrices, operational assumptions, source-case provenance, paired ro-
                 bustness diagnostics and replay procedures for the shared-review study. It separates the original maintenance
                 distributions from constructed competition, and separates fresh retail source cases from post hoc analysis of
                 earlier preparations. Detailed outcome tables retain failures and blocked unresolved requests. The main paper
                 contains the formulation, central comparisons, uncertainty and limitations needed to assess its claims. This
                 supplement provides additional inspection and reproduction detail. It is a local research companion and is not
                 represented as an accepted supplementary submission under the conference policy.
